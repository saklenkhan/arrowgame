Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

App Name: Arrow Escape (Arrow Puzzle) • Package ID: com.whitetech.arrow.puzzle.game

Effective Date: October 24, 2024 • Last Updated: October 24, 2024

This Privacy Policy explains how WhiteTech ("we", "our", or "us") manages your information when you download, install, and use the mobile application Arrow Escape (listed as Arrow Puzzle on Google Play, package name: com.whitetech.arrow.puzzle.game, hereinafter referred to as the "App").

By using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Zero Direct Personal Data Collection: We do not require an account, username, email, phone number, or social media login to play the game.

We do not collect, process, or sell any of the following personal information:

  • Real name, postal address, email, or telephone numbers.
  • Financial, payment, or credit card information.
  • Precise GPS location data.
  • Camera, microphone, audio recording, or phonebook/contact permissions.

2. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

Arrow Escape is engineered to be an offline-first game. The App creates and stores gameplay state purely on your local device storage using a local key-value database (Hive):

  • Current unlocked level and completed stage progress.
  • Best scores, solve times, and daily challenge streaks.
  • Custom skin selections and cosmetic theme preferences.
  • Gameplay settings (sound effects, background music, haptic vibrations, infinite lives toggle).

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server owned by us. You can permanently delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To support ongoing development and offer the game for free, Arrow Escape integrates third-party mobile advertising services.

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

We use Google AdMob to deliver banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and device identifiers in accordance with Google's policies, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), hardware model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geolocation inferred from your IP address to deliver regionally appropriate ads and protect against ad fraud.
  • Diagnostics & Ad Performance: Impression counters, click interactions, app responsiveness, and diagnostic error reports.

To learn more about how Google processes information, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure for Google Play

4. User Privacy Rights & Consent Controls

GDPR (EEA & UK Users)

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we implement Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your explicit consent regarding personalized ads and device identifier processing. You can revisit and alter your consent choices at any time directly inside the App by opening Settings > PRIVACY & ADS > Ad Privacy & Consent.

CCPA / CPRA (California Residents)

We do not sell personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Users can opt out of interest-based ad profiling at the device level.

Device-Level Advertising Controls

You can reset or disable your Android Advertising ID at any time:

  • Open Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • Select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • You can also customize your Google advertising preferences at Google Ad Settings.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Arrow Escape is intended for a general audience of casual puzzle fans. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable EU territories). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information without proper consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate measures to ensure that any associated data is permanently purged.

6. Security of Your Information

We are dedicated to safeguarding any data processed in connection with the App. All communication between the Google Mobile Ads SDK and advertising networks is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption protocols. Local game save files remain isolated within Android's protected application sandbox.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local gameplay data: Stored on your device until you clear the app cache/data via device settings or delete/uninstall the App.
  • Ad service telemetry: Handled and automatically aged out according to Google's standard data retention policies.

8. Updates to this Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to conform with new legal standards or feature additions. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date. Continued usage of the App implies acceptance of the revised policy.
